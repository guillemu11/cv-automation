## Design Context

### Users
Job seekers actively searching for work — non-technical professionals (marketing managers, brand managers, account managers). They use the dashboard daily, typically in the morning, to review new job matches and prepare application materials. The primary user is Paula De Francisco, a Brand & Marketing Manager in Dubai. She trusts the system's output completely and won't edit generated documents — everything must be send-ready.

### Brand Personality
Capable, precise, quietly confident. Like having a sharp executive recruiter working for you 24/7. Not flashy or startup-y — this is a serious tool for serious job seekers.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Refined dark command center** — the feeling of a Bloomberg terminal crossed with Linear.app. Dark mode is correct: users check this in the evening or early morning, often reviewing many items in one session. Dense information display but never cluttered. Tier colors (Hot red, Warm amber, Cold blue) are the dominant visual language — every job is an "asset" in a portfolio of opportunities.

**Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards with purple gradients. Notion-like tools that feel like blank canvases. Anything that looks like "yet another project management tool."

**References**: Linear.app (density + clarity), Superhuman (focused, fast), Bloomberg Terminal (information-rich dark UI), Raycast (refined dark, purposeful).

### Design Principles
1. **Zero friction to value** — Paula opens the app and sees results, not configuration. Every click should move her closer to applying.
2. **Information density without noise** — Show scores, tiers, skills, files all at once. But through visual hierarchy, not by dumping text.
3. **Documents are first-class** — PDFs (CV, CL, Form Responses, Deliverables) are the core output. Download buttons should be prominent, not buried.
4. **Copy-paste is a feature** — Outreach email copy and LinkedIn messages need one-click copy. This is where the rubber meets the road.
5. **The board tells the story** — The Kanban view is the user's narrative: "I found 5 Hot jobs, generated materials for 3, applied to 2, got 1 interview." Progress should feel tangible.

## Design Context — Landings (external deliverables)

The sections above govern the **internal dashboard**. The system also emits
external artifacts the user sends to third parties: the most ambitious is the
**landing-page deliverable** under `output/<Company_Role>/landing/`, a
self-contained HTML+CSS+JS bundle attached to speculative cold-outreach. It
is a fundamentally different design problem from the dashboard and has its
own context.

### Users (landing surface)

**Reader (primary):** mid-to-senior fashion-retail hiring managers and creative
directors in Dubai / wider MENA. They open the link cold from email or
LinkedIn — three seconds to decide whether to keep scrolling or close. Most
will view first on a phone in transit, then forward to desktop if interested.

**Sender:** Paula De Francisco. The landing is her *taste sample*, not her
strategy document. The reader has her CV separately for the substance.

**Job to be done:** make the reader feel that whoever produced this landing
*belongs in the brand room* of a premium fashion retailer. Aesthetic
credibility is the deliverable; strategy is a bonus.

### Brand Personality (landing surface)

**Three words:** *editorial, confident, restrained*.

**Voice:** the way a senior fashion-business strategist speaks in print —
short declarative sentences, no hedging, no buzzwords ("synergies",
"leverage", "best-in-class" are banned). When in doubt, less.

**Emotional goal:** the reader closes the tab thinking *"who made this, let's
talk"* — not *"good points 1 through 6."*

**Anti-personality (hard nos):**
- Not consultancy-deck (no "key takeaways," no exec-summary boxes)
- Not influencer-creator-portfolio (no neon, no maximalism, no auto-play video)
- Not corporate-careers-page (no smiling-team photos, no values pillars)
- Not AI-template (no centered hero+subtitle, no 3-column features, no
  gradient backgrounds, no testimonial cards, no border-left accent stripes,
  no gradient text)

### Aesthetic Direction (landing surface)

**Primary reference:** **AnOther Magazine** layout discipline + **SSENSE
editorial** photography rhythm. Asymmetric grids, generous whitespace, captions
that earn their place, typography as intervention rather than decoration.

**Accent reference:** **Off-White-style** typographic statements for
pull-quotes — short uppercase phrases acting as visual punctuation. *Not* a
full Off-White cosplay (no diagonal stripes, no quote-marks-around-everything).

**Hard anti-references:** Vogue.com (too centered, too cover-magazine), Drest
(too gamified, too animated), Cosmopolitan-style (too lifestyle), Pinterest
"brand book" templates (literally the AI training set).

**Theme:** **light surface** — warm paper. One inverted (ink) section near
the closing. Avoid full-dark; Dubai readers default to bright daylight viewing.

**Photography:** generated through Higgsfield Soul Cinematic, 2k. Editorial
fashion-magazine register, no logos, no faces close enough to be recognisable.

### Typography (landing surface ONLY — overrides the system default)

The CV and cover letter retain Space Grotesk + Inter for ATS legibility. The
landing **deliberately departs** because both are reflex AI fonts; using them
here would undercut the "taste sample" thesis.

- **Display (h1, h2, pull-quotes):** **Italiana** (Google Fonts) — high-
  contrast didone, extreme fashion-magazine feel
- **Secondary display weight:** **Bodoni Moda** — classical didone, for h3 /
  large captions if Italiana proves too thin at smaller sizes
- **Body + UI labels:** **Geist** — modern grotesk that avoids the Inter
  silhouette; small-caps with wide tracking for eyebrows / kickers

**Pairing decision: Italiana + Geist.** Type scale modular, ratio 1.333 on a
16px body, six named steps (eyebrow / body / lede / h3 / h2 / h1). Fluid
sizing via `clamp()` for h1 and h2 only. Body capped at 56ch.

### Color (landing surface)

OKLCH only, tinted neutrals, no pure black or white:

- `--ink: oklch(0.18 0.01 60)` — near-black, warm-tinted
- `--paper: oklch(0.97 0.008 80)` — primary surface, warm cream
- `--paper-deep: oklch(0.94 0.012 70)` — secondary surface for inset blocks
- `--accent: oklch(0.55 0.14 35)` — burnt sienna / dried rose. Replaces
  champagne gold (gold is the fashion-AI cliché). Sienna reads editorial,
  expensive, from-earth.
- `--muted: oklch(0.55 0.005 60)` — neutral mid-grey, slight warm tint

**Discipline:** 80% paper, 15% ink, 5% accent. Accent appears in **exactly
three places per landing**: one pull-quote, one underline-on-hover, one
stats-block emphasis. Never as a button background. No gradients anywhere.

### Layout Strategy (landing surface)

**Hard reset.** The previous 6-identical-sections-with-figure-aside model is
killed. Replaced by **8 blocks of deliberately distinct shape:**

1. **Hero** — full-bleed photograph, headline overlaid bottom-left, no scroll
   hint, no kicker noise
2. **Pull-quote** — single typographic statement on paper, Italiana italic,
   max 14 words. Off-White-style attribution underneath in tiny tracked
   uppercase
3. **Diptych** — two photographs side-by-side, ONE 4-word caption between them
4. **Manifesto** — single paragraph (max 50 words) on the left, one portrait
   photo on the right. Asymmetric: text 5/12, photo 4/12, gutter 3/12 of
   intentional empty space
5. **Full-bleed image** — one photograph, full viewport, no text. Respiration
6. **Triptych** — three small photos in a row, each with one tight caption
7. **Stats** — three numbers, each on its own line, accent-colored, label in
   tiny tracked uppercase. NO surrounding card/box
8. **Closing** — inverted (ink background, paper text). One short paragraph,
   one CTA line, contact strip

**Total text per landing:** target ~80 words, hard ceiling 120. The previous
draft was ~600.

### Design Principles (landing surface)

1. **Photography earns its size.** A photograph larger than its caption pays
   for the size by being interesting alone. If it doesn't, shrink it or kill it.
2. **Whitespace is content.** Empty space is the most expensive thing on the
   page. Don't fill it.
3. **One typographic gesture per block.** Either serif headline, sans
   eyebrow, or italic quote — not all three. Restraint reads as taste.
4. **Asymmetry over symmetry.** If two elements could be centered, push one
   to the gutter. Center is the AI default. Refuse it.
5. **No skeuomorphic decoration.** No pixel quote marks pretending to be
   ink, no scribble underlines, no torn-paper edges, no fake stamps. The work
   doesn't need props.

### Motion (landing surface)

- Reveals: opacity-only fade + 12px translate-up, 600ms `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.3, 1)`
- ONE choreographed entrance: hero headline lines stagger in 100ms apart
- Hover micro-interactions: links and CTAs ONLY, color shift only, never scale
- No parallax. No scroll-linked motion beyond the IntersectionObserver trigger
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → all motion disabled
