The insight

Roughly 88% of UAE residents are expats, and the country is one of the world's largest remittance hubs. For millions of people here, "money" isn't local — it's a monthly journey to Kerala, Manila, Cairo or London. Yet the world's average remittance still costs around 6% and can take days.

Revolut already solves this. The brand opportunity isn't a new feature — it's to own the cultural truth that, in the UAE, your money was never meant to stop at the border.

How I'd run it as Brand & Growth Manager, UAE

01

Positioning

One idea, defensible and local: Revolut = money with no borders. Own the corridor before challengers do.

02

Audience & message

Segment by corridor (India, Philippines, Egypt, Pakistan, UK) and life-stage; tailor message, creator and channel per nuance.

03

Full-funnel GTM

Acquisition in the corridor → activation on first transfer → retention in daily multi-currency spend. One calendar, global playbook.

04

Test & scale

Hypotheses, A/B on creative and offer, ROAS discipline — then operationalise the winners into a regional playbook.

The manifesto

You crossed a border to build a life here.

Your money crosses one every single month — to the people who made you.

So why does it still queue, wait days, and lose a cut on the way?

With Revolut, the border was never there. Send it home before the exchange desk even opens.

The campaign, across the funnel

Six touchpoints — each mapped to a funnel stage a Marketing Manager would own end-to-end.

Awareness
OOH · Sheikh Zayed Rd & Metro

"Home is 4,000 km and 3 seconds away."

Corridor-specific billboards & Metro wraps — a different destination city per line, timed to payday week.

Awareness
Social · corridor creators

The people who send it home

Micro-creators from each community (Malayali, Filipino, Egyptian, Pakistani) tell the real story of sending money — UGC, not gloss.

Consideration
Content · the rate, exposed

"See what the desk didn't tell you."

Data-driven stories comparing real cost of a transfer vs. Revolut — the conversion engine of the campaign.

Conversion
In-app · first-transfer GTM

Zero-fee first send

Localised onboarding & a payday-week offer engineered to drive the single activation metric that matters: the first transfer.

Retention
CRM · beyond the border

From one transfer to every day

Lifecycle EDM & push nudging multi-currency spend, savings vaults and travel — turning a remitter into a daily user.

Growth loop
Referral · the corridor multiplies

One sender, a whole community

Community referral engineered around how these networks actually share — a compounding, low-CAC growth loop.
