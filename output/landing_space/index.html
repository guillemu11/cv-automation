Global brands.Local skin.

A perfume becomes a bestseller the moment it smells like belonging. Local Notes is a market-by-market system for turning global fragrance houses into local rituals across Africa.

The insight
It's not translation.

Global fragrance loses in African markets when it arrives as a translated ad and a one-size price. It wins when scent, ritual and price tier are localised by market — the same focus-then-scale discipline behind onboarding 30+ fragrance stores in two months and growing 42 beauty & fragrance accounts +30% GMV QoQ.

The manifesto

Scent has no borders — but it has a home.

Lagos is not Nairobi. Casablanca is not Accra. A note that signals luxury in one city signals nothing in the next.

Local Notes treats each market as its own launch: a scent family that already lives there, a price tier the shelf can hold, and a story told by people that market already trusts. Global equity, earned locally — one country at a time.

Campaign executions

01

The Scent Map

A per-country positioning grid — scent family × price tier × hero SKU — so every market leads with the fragrance most likely to repeat, not the global default.

02

Local Voices

Micro-creator "scent diaries" per market: trusted local faces translating a global house into everyday ritual. Briefed to hero SKUs, tracked to sell-out — not reach.

03

The Sampling Ritual

Discovery is everything in fragrance. Seeding and sampling across modern trade and quick-commerce turn trial intent into shelf conversion, market by market.

04

Shelf Theatre

Perfect-store fragrance bays with local cues — so the global brand looks premium and feels native at the exact moment of choice.
