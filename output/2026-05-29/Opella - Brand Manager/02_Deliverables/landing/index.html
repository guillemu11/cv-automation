01 — the gap

Healthcare brands face unique trust barriers in UAE market

Opella's OTC portfolio — Allegra, Buscopan, Dulcolax — operates in a market where consumers research symptoms in Arabic but find brand communication in English. The disconnect is measurable.

UAE healthcare advertising requires MOHAP compliance, Ramadan-sensitive messaging, and cultural adaptation that generic FMCG playbooks miss. Consumer trust builds through local relevance, not global templates.

02 — the answer

Build healthcare brands like culture-first consumer products

Healthcare marketing succeeds when brands speak to daily life, not clinical features. UAE consumers choose Panadol over alternatives because the brand understands local pain points — work stress, weather changes, social obligations.
