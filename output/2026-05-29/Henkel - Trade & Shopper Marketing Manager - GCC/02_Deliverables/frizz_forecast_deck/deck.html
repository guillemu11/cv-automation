01 · Shopper & category insight — GCC
Hair damage here is
a daily, weather event.
  • Climate-driven need: heat, sun & humidity 8+ months/year make damage, frizz and scalp care year-round needs — Schwarzkopf can own "repair & protect" (Gliss) and premium care (BC Bonacure).
  • Two shoppers, two missions: high-frequency replenishment in grocery/hyper vs. advice-led discovery in pharmacy.
  • Premium + value polarity: a premium expat segment and a value mass segment coexist — the range must serve both without trading down.
  • Channels that matter: Modern Trade (Carrefour/MAF, Lulu, Union Coop, Spinneys), pharmacy (BinSina, Aster, Life), e-tail (Noon, Amazon.ae), quick-commerce (Noon Minutes, Talabat).
  • Seasonal peaks: summer anti-frizz, Ramadan & Eid gifting, back-to-school, DSF/GITEX.

02 · How it works
One index. Two layers.

Weather

Humidity + UV, the number every GCC shopper already checks daily.

Frizz Index 0–100

Published weekly threshold → LOW / MED / HIGH.

Pre-merchandised answer

Repair & protect block becomes the obvious fix.

In-store · KAM-sellable

A weekly swap, not a gimmick

  • Repair endcap ships with 3 pre-printed headers — LOW / MED / HIGH. Each Monday the published index says which is live.
  • On HIGH weeks: swap to the HIGH header + a humidity price-pack bundle (Gliss shampoo+mask).
  • New Perfect Store audit line: "Frizz block compliance" (header + correct tier + bundle faced).
Online · genuinely live

Automated where it can be

  • E-tail "Today's Frizz Index" badge + search bids that scale on HIGH days.
  • Q-commerce weather-triggered tiles + one-tap "Frizz Rescue" bundle.
  • Retail-media display bids up on HIGH days — ROAS measurable against the index.
