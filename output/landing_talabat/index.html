Full Plate
A key-account growth concept · Restaurant partnerships

Every partner,a bestseller.

Full Plate is an account-growth system that treats every key restaurant partner like the bestseller it could become — owned, measured, and grown on the platform, not just managed.

Scroll
The insight
Partners don't churn over commission.

They churn when nobody owns their growth. The accounts that thrive on a platform are the ones with a manager who segments by potential, leads from their data, and turns renewals into a growth conversation — the same discipline behind 42 accounts at +30% GMV QoQ.

The manifesto

A restaurant on the platform isn't a line item. It's a business with a P&L, a kitchen, and a ceiling no one has helped it break.

Full Plate gives the top accounts what they actually need to grow: a clear tier, a shared dashboard, and a manager who shows up with the next move already mapped. Marketing, product, ops and finance pulling in one direction — per account, every quarter.

How it works

01

Tier by potential

Map every key account on GMV potential × primary growth lever (menu, pricing, visibility, ops reliability). Concentrate effort where it compounds, not evenly across the book.

02

Lead from the data

A shared per-account dashboard — GMV, conversion, prep time, promo ROI. Every review starts from evidence, so renewals become joint growth plans, not haggling.

03

Cross-functional sprints

Each major account initiative runs as a small project with owners across marketing, ops and finance — so a promo ships with the ops readiness and visibility to actually convert.

04

Renewal as growth

Commercial terms framed around the next 12 months of mutual growth — backed by the data that proves the partnership is working for both sides.
