# talabat — Sr. Specialist Retail Media Insight
## Campo del formulario: "How does your current skillset match the role you are applying for?"

### Versión larga (~250 palabras · ~1.700 caracteres)

I come to retail media from the advertiser side, which means I already work with the levers
this role is built to diagnose. At DoFreeze I plan, buy and optimise retail media on talabat,
Noon and Careem — alongside Meta and Google — across 50+ markets, reading ROAS, conversion and
incremental sell-out to reallocate budget mid-flight and flag under-delivering activity before
the window closes.

Measurement is where I add the most. I ran SMASH × talabat against Befit as a control brand on
the same platform and in the same window: +165% daily revenue versus +4.7% on the control, about
AED 32K incremental revenue on roughly AED 8.7K of spend. On Noon, Befit delivered +4,176
incremental units, +31% over baseline. I also audited an agency's campaign reporting and found
reach estimated from follower counts, "unique accounts" summed from estimates, and the same
creator double-counted across waves with 5× variance — then rebuilt the KPI definitions and
moved spend into cohorts outperforming campaign average by 10–40×. That is the standardising
measurement frameworks and aligning KPI definitions part of this role, already done in practice.

Behind that sits FMCG grounding (category planning at Mondelez with Nielsen: sell-in/sell-out
and promotional effectiveness), commercial ownership on the platform side at Miravia (Alibaba),
where I ran Flash Sales against P&L targets with revenue pacing and corrective action on
shortfalls, and a working BI stack — Power BI, Tableau, Looker, advanced Excel modelling and
AI-automated dashboards covering 50+ markets.

What I would be learning is the platform's internal view of monetisation and ad inventory. What
I bring instead is the perspective of the partner whose budget that revenue depends on.
