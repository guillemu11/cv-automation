## How does your current skillset match the role you are applying for?

### Versión completa (~1.250 caracteres)

I already do a smaller version of this job, and part of it happens on talabat.

At DoFreeze in Dubai I plan three food brands around occasions: Fitness Month, New Year New Me, Ramadan and back to school. Every campaign, creator wave and partnership is built around that calendar. I choose partners for the audience they bring: an in-app giveaway with talabat cashback cards, running, padel and yoga communities, and a creator programme I built from zero (103 activations over three campaigns).

The part I'm proudest of is proving that brand work sells. For SMASH on talabat I used a sister brand as a control group: daily sales rose 165%, against 4.7% for the control. On Noon, New Year New Me added 4,176 incremental units.

My team of two (a designer and a social media executive) works with AI every day. I built a Claude-based system for planning, content and reporting that cut our manual work by about 40%, and an AI avatar that explains our content brief to creators in several languages. It's the same idea as the brand tools you want for markets and agencies, at a smaller size.

I've also moved across categories and matrix organisations: chocolate at Mondelez, beauty and fashion at Alibaba's Miravia, and food delivery at Glovo, inside the Delivery Hero group.
