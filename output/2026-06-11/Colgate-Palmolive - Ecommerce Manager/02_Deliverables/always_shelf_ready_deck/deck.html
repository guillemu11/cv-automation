01 · The digital-shelf truth — UAE
The category is won
on the first screen.
  • Decided at search: ~7 in 10 e-commerce purchases are won at the title, image, rating and price the shopper sees first — long before checkout.
  • A crowded shelf: on Noon & Amazon.ae, oral & personal care is a search-share battle won by content, ratings and availability.
  • Two missions: planned replenishment on marketplaces vs. 10-minute impulse on quick-commerce (Noon Minutes, Talabat, Careem).
  • Retail media is the new profit pool: the fastest-growing FMCG lever — but only with ROAS discipline.
  • So the job is clear: make every hero SKU perfect on the digital shelf, every day, and prove it in the P&L.
