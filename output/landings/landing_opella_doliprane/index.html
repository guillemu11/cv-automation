Insight film · Diaspora kitchens
The drawer everyone has

A short documentary series filmed inside 12 real Dubai apartments — Lebanese, Tunisian, Moroccan, Algerian, French. Each opens the same drawer. Each has the same box. Aired as a Vimeo OTT campaign + cinema preroll in DIFC + Mall of the Emirates Vox screens.

OOH + Search · 5 MENA cities
Where the word still means paracetamol

Search-data billboards in five cities (Beirut, Tunis, Casablanca, Algiers, Dubai), each updating live with that day's local search interest for the word "Doliprane". A reveal moment for an audience that thought they were the only one.

5 cities · 8-week dynamic OOH
Retail launch · UAE
Now in UAE pharmacies

Coordinated retail launch in BinSina (160 stores), Boots Middle East (90), Aster (200) and Life Pharmacy (180). End-cap takeover in week 1: a single yellow-orange box next to a handwritten paper tag — "Now in UAE."

E-commerce · Amazon.ae + Noon Daily
The PDP that ends the suitcase era

A premium PDP build: the original French packaging, an "Imported from France" badge, and an Arabic-French bilingual product description. A hero rail on Amazon.ae's pharmacy department for the first 60 days.

Cultural anchor · 30s film + social cuts
One ritual, three generations

A 30-second hero film documenting the moment a grandmother passes a Doliprane to a grandchild — the most universally remembered "first medicine" memory across Lebanese, Tunisian, Moroccan and Algerian households. Cut into 6-second social verticals for IG Reels + TikTok.

OOH · Paris CDG · Terminal 2E
Catch the suitcase before it leaves Paris

DOOH at the Paris–Dubai boarding gates: "Don't pack it in your suitcase anymore. It's already on Amazon.ae." Targets the exact moment the diaspora was about to import Doliprane by hand. Closes the loop with a QR jumping straight to the live UAE PDP.
