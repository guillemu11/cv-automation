The manifesto

For thirty years Buscopan lived behind a counter.

But at one in the morning, the only thing open in Dubai is a rider with a thermal bag.

Treat the rider like a pharmacist. Treat the app like a shelf. Treat the late-hour cramp like the brand moment it has been all along.

Twelve dirhams, twenty-eight minutes, one blister.

Campaign executions

Six moves across the UAE quick-commerce stack — Talabat, Noon Daily, Careem Quik, Deliveroo HOP — plus a retail anchor, a telehealth upsell and late-night DOOH.

Retail anchor · 240 UAE pharmacies
The shelf that points to your phone

From 22:00, a single LED strip lights only the Buscopan row. A vinyl sticker on the shelf base reads "Closing? We're not." with a QR straight to the Noon Daily / Talabat order link, prefilled.

240 night-shift pharmacies · 60-day pilot
Brand partnership · Talabat Grocery + Careem Quik
The late-night pharmacy run

Co-branded a curated "After-Hours Relief" rail inside Talabat Grocery and Careem Quik, surfaced from 22:00 only. Buscopan is the hero SKU with a guaranteed 30-min ETA in Dubai + Sharjah + Abu Dhabi.

3 emirates · 22:00–02:00 daypart · 6-month commit
Digital · Noon Daily + Deliveroo HOP
The 12-AED, 28-minute promise

A single price-and-time hero card. Twelve dirhams, twenty-eight minutes, one blister. No bundling, no banner ads — just the most direct promise a CHC brand can make in a quick-commerce app.

UAE · always-on after 22:00 · A/B test vs Tylenol/Telfast
CRM · subscription + re-order
"Reorder by 22:30" Friday push

Smart re-order nudge to the cohort that bought Buscopan after 23:00 the previous week — fired at 22:30 on the day they previously bought. Reframes a category from impulse to dependable.

CRM build · LTV target +28% over 12 months
Telehealth · in-app · 23:00+
The pharmacist who never closes

Every Buscopan order placed after 23:00 unlocks a one-tap chat with a licensed pharmacist (Mumzworld / Maaref partnerships). Symptoms longer than 24h escalate into a same-night teleconsultation, opening a second monetisation surface for Opella.

UAE pilot · 90 days · co-branded with a telehealth partner
DOOH · Dubai Metro · late-night
Pharmacy closed. We're not.

Vertical DOOH screens across 12 Metro stations, programmed to run only between 22:30 and 02:00. The tagline anchors offline awareness at the exact moment the consumer is most likely to need the product. QR routes straight to the in-app Buscopan card.

12 stations · 4-week burst · Dubai + Sharjah
