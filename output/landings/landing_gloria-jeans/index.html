05 — Go-to-Market

Three phases.
Eighteen months.

A staged entry that builds digital demand before committing to physical retail — reducing risk while maximizing launch impact.

Phase 1 Months 1–6

Digital First

  • Launch e-commerce on gloria-jeans.ae with Noon/Namshi integration
  • TikTok + Instagram brand accounts — content engine from day one
  • Influencer seeding: 30 micro-influencers across 5 communities (Emirati, South Asian, Arab, Western, Filipino)
  • Modest fashion capsule collection — test demand before physical retail
Phase 2 Months 6–12

Flagship Launch

  • Flagship store in a Tier 1 mall (Dubai Hills Mall or City Centre Deira — high footfall, lower rent than Dubai Mall)
  • Experiential retail: fitting rooms with social sharing, QR-to-buy, click & collect
  • PR launch event with regional fashion media
  • Performance marketing scale-up: Google Shopping, Meta ads, TikTok Shop
Phase 3 Months 12–18

Mall Rollout + GCC

  • 3–5 additional mall locations across Dubai and Abu Dhabi
  • Jebel Ali hub fully operational for GCC distribution
  • Loyalty programme with Noon integration
  • Saudi Arabia market assessment — the $32B opportunity next door
06 — Channel Strategy

Meet them where they live.

E-Commerce

Own site + Noon + Namshi. Fashion is 21.6% of UAE online spend. Mobile-first — 80% of transactions on mobile.

Social Commerce

TikTok Shop + IG Shoppable Posts. $3.2B market growing 15%+ annually. Short-form video is the #1 fashion discovery channel.

Influencer Engine

30 micro-influencers across 5 communities. 75% of UAE consumers trust influencer recommendations. Focus on haul + GRWM formats.

Physical Retail

Mall culture is not dead in UAE — 98% occupancy rates. Experiential flagship first, then targeted rollout to Tier 1 & 2 malls.

07 — From Dubai to MENA

Dubai is the launchpad.
MENA is the destination.

Gloria Jeans already has the Jebel Ali supply chain hub. Dubai sits at the geographic centre of a region with 400M+ consumers and $7.3B in fashion spend across the GCC alone. Saudi Arabia — with its $32B fashion market and Vision 2030 tailwinds — is the natural next step.

Campaign Concept

Everybody's wardrobe.
Nobody's costume.

The campaign follows five real residents of Dubai — from five different backgrounds — through a single day. Same city. Same heat. Same hustle. Different lives. All wearing Gloria Jeans. The clothes aren't the story. The people are. The clothes just work.

L
Layla, 28
Emirati entrepreneur. Runs a home bakery. Needs modest, breathable, camera-ready looks for her daily Instagram Lives.
R
Raj, 34
Indian software engineer. Cycles to work in JLT. Needs office-ready fits that survive 42C commutes.
M
Maria, 26
Filipino nurse. Works night shifts at Rashid Hospital. Needs affordable, durable basics she can rely on seven days a week.
K
Karl, 31
German teacher. Lives in Al Barsha. Needs casual weekend wear that's appropriate for mall culture and brunch.
F
Fatima, 22
Jordanian university student. Studies at AUS Sharjah. Needs trend-forward modest pieces that don't break her budget.
