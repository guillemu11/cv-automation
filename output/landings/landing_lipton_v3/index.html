The journey

Same tea. Seventeen homes.

For too long, the industry has treated local bottler variation as a problem to be solved — a compromise against one perfect global formula.

We think it's the opposite. The variation is the feature. Seventeen recipes, tuned to seventeen waters, seventeen palates, seventeen suns. Seventeen reasons Lipton tastes like home — no matter which home you're in.

Campaign executions

Six touchpoints that make the seventeen-bottler story visible to the consumer.

OOH · Regional
A billboard per bottler

Seventeen OOH creatives, one per bottler city. Each reads the local tea's signature in the local language — "Brighter than Jebel Ali's water", "Rounder than Nile's minerals". Same font, same typography, seventeen stories.

17 cities · AMESA-wide · 6-month run
Social · TikTok
#WhereIsYourLipton

Creators in each bottler city film a 15-second clip uncapping their local Lipton at a landmark. One hashtag, seventeen homes. Users participate by posting their own with location geotag.

Primary KPI: 80M views, 17 city trends
Retail · Modern Trade
The Bottler Badge

Each bottle gets a discreet cap-ring badge naming the bottler that produced it. No extra cost — just a rotating SKU sticker. Consumers start noticing which bottler's version they prefer.

220+ stores · 17 markets · always on
Digital · Delivery apps
"Your local Lipton" badge

In Talabat, Careem, foodpanda, the Lipton product card carries a soft "Bottled in your city" micro-badge. Trusts the bottler, signals freshness, differentiates from competitor RTD teas.

45-day pilot · UAE, KSA, PAK, EGY
Experiential · Ramadan
Iftar, your home.

Sponsored majlis series across the 17 cities, each with its locally-bottled Lipton as the first drink poured at sunset. Content captured and broadcast across the network.

Ramadan 1448 · 17 cities · region-wide
Creator · Docu-series
The 17 (mini-docs)

A six-episode docu-series visiting bottlers across AMESA. Factory-floor access, plant-managers as characters, tied to YouTube + TikTok long-form. Built-in paid-media amplification on each episode.

6 episodes · 6-month run · AMESA + global
