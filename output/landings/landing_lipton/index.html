The manifesto

For fifty years, iced tea was something your parents drank on a patio.

We think it belongs in your hand at three in the afternoon, when Dubai is 42°, your phone is dying, and a can of something sweet would be the wrong answer.

So we cut the sugar in half. We kept the Rainforest Alliance leaves. We doubled down on the sun.

The afternoon just got lighter.

Now watch it happen

Half the sugar,
everywhere.

Six touchpoints. One afternoon. Six markets.

Campaign executions

Six touchpoints across the AMESA region.

OOH · Sheikh Zayed Rd
A billboard that fades in the sun

Photochromic ink. The tagline "Half the sugar" is invisible at dawn and fully visible at solar noon. Dubai's own light powers the ad.

Dubai · Abu Dhabi · Riyadh
Social · TikTok
#HalfTheSugarChallenge

Creators swap one sugary afternoon drink for Lipton Ice Tea for 7 days. AR filter detects their "sun exposure" and colours the video progressively golden.

Primary KPI: 50M views, 40% Gen Z reach
In-store · Carrefour AMESA
The 3pm Cooler

A dedicated afternoon-refresh cooler zone, yellow-lit, stocked only after 2pm. Takes the "afternoon occasion" from colas.

220 stores · 4 markets · 3-month pilot
Digital · Delivery apps
Free with every 3pm order

Partner with Talabat, Careem, Noon Food. Any food delivery placed between 3–6pm adds a free Lipton Ice Tea can. Data backflow feeds the AMESA DMP.

45-day pilot · UAE + KSA
Experiential · Ramadan
Iftar, reset.

The first sip after iftar is never water alone. Lipton Ice Tea sponsors "iftar reset" micro-moments across Majlis and cafés — single-serve 250ml cans, half-sugar only.

Ramadan 1448 · region-wide
Creator · Influencer
7 afternoons, 7 cities

Seven AMESA creators document one "half-the-sugar afternoon" in their home city. Dubai, Cairo, Karachi, Amman, Jeddah, Casablanca, Lagos.

Always-on series · 6-month run
