The insight
Sub-heroes don't flop. They get the hero's hand-me-downs.

Most sub-hero launches inherit a scaled-down version of the hero playbook — and underperform for it. Give them their own fit-for-purpose motion — creator-led, e-commerce-first, conversion-tracked — and the supporting cast starts selling like a lead.

The manifesto

Beauty is bought on discovery, not on reach.

A community-first brand doesn't need every launch to be loud — it needs every launch to be true to the product and trackable to sell-out.

Sub-Hero is one brief, one truth, one consistent story across messaging, paid, in-store and e-commerce — with creators briefed to the SKU and measured to conversion, not impressions.

The playbook

01

Tiered launch motions

A distinct sub-hero playbook — tighter creator set, owned & e-commerce content led, lighter paid — so sub-heroes launch fit-for-purpose instead of inheriting a hero's budget logic.

02

Creator-to-conversion

Creators briefed against the launch SKU with trackable links and post-campaign sell-out analysis — turning influencer spend from awareness into measurable revenue.

03

One brief, one truth

Every touchpoint — messaging, paid, in-store, e-commerce, PR — ships from a single brief and asset tracker, with a cross-functional check before go-live. No drift across markets.

04

E-commerce-first

Sub-heroes launch where they convert: PDP content, bundles and quick-commerce visibility built in from day one, not bolted on after the hero moment passes.
