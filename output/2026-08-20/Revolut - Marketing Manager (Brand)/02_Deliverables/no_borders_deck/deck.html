01 · The insight — UAE
88%
of UAE residents are expats — one of the world's largest remittance hubs
Here, money is
born to cross
a border.
  • For millions of people, "money" isn't local — it's a monthly journey to Kerala, Manila, Cairo, Lahore or London.
  • Yet the world's average remittance still costs around 6% and can take days — much of it still sent in cash, in a queue.
  • Revolut already solves this. The brand opportunity isn't a feature — it's to own the cultural truth that your money was never meant to stop at the border.

03 · How it works
One story, the whole funnel.
Awareness

Own the corridor

Community-true OOH & creators make Revolut the name attached to "sending home".

Consideration

Expose the rate

Data-driven stories show the real cost of the old way vs. Revolut.

Conversion

First transfer

Localised onboarding + a payday-week offer engineered for one activation metric.

Retention

Everyday money

From one transfer to multi-currency spend, savings and travel — a daily habit.

Brand layer · earns attention

A cultural truth, not an ad

  • One line — "send it home before the exchange desk opens" — carried across OOH, social and creators, adapted per corridor.
  • Tone of voice & style guide owned locally; global assets adapted to real UAE nuance.
Growth layer · drives results

Engineered for the metric

  • Paid social & app-install campaigns A/B-tested on creative & offer; scaled on ROAS.
  • Winners operationalised into a regional playbook so the idea scales without re-inventing it.
