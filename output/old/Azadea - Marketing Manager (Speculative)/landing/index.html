01 — the gap

Investindustrial commits €100m. The story stays small.

February's Eataly restructuring signals board-level belief: €100m for 40 stores, Investindustrial taking direct control. Yet the narrative remains tactical — more locations, bigger footprint.

The real opportunity sits untapped. F&B delivers structurally higher visit frequency than apparel. Eataly customers who discover Zara through cross-format programming become higher-value, stickier customers before the IPO restarts. Said Daher champions experiential retail, but the formats still operate in silos.

02 — the thesis

Build the GCC's first true lifestyle ecosystem

Azadea has the pieces: premium F&B (Eataly, Beihouse), fast fashion authority, beauty destination status through Sephora MENA. The missing element is orchestration — programming that makes the customer journey flow between formats, creating a lifestyle position no competitor can replicate.

03 — the plan

Three phases. Six months.

Turn Eataly's €100m momentum into cross-format customer acquisition and retention engine.

  1. 01 Months 1-2

    Build the foundation

    Unified loyalty architecture connecting Eataly visits to Zara purchase data. Launch Italian seasonal programming — olive harvest workshops, truffle tastings — that drive fashion customers into F&B and vice versa. Establish data flows between formats.

  2. 02 Months 3-4

    Launch cross-format

    Deploy Ramadan iftar series at Eataly locations with Azadea fashion partnerships. Create 'Italian Week' shopping events where Eataly purchases unlock Zara early access. Build repeat-visit cadence through monthly experiential programming that spans food and fashion.

  3. 03 Months 5-6

    Scale the ecosystem

    Roll out learnings to Saudi market ahead of Riyadh Eataly opening. Launch subscription-style loyalty tier for high-frequency customers. Create exportable playbook for new market entries that positions Azadea as lifestyle destination, not just multibrand operator.
