01 — the gap

Dubai stores launch into a MENA social vacuum

Gloria Jeans' MENA presence reads like an afterthought. The Oman Instagram handle sits at under 300 followers. The global account posts exclusively in Russian. Zero Arabic content, zero local creator partnerships, zero Ramadan or Eid storytelling.

Meanwhile, VP Ecommerce Lionel Boukobza arrives with 12 years of Stradivarius MENA experience. Dubai-based international operations are scaling. There is a six-month runway sitting unused before the first stores open.

02 — the answer

Build the brand before the store opens

Every successful MENA fast-fashion launch follows the same playbook: arrive in malls with awareness, not as another unknown logo. Zara didn't wait for foot traffic to build brand heat. Neither should Gloria Jeans.

03 — the plan

Three phases. Six months.

Arabic-first social foundation, UAE creator network, then market-ready brand presence at store launch.

  1. 01 Months 1-2

    Build the foundation

    Launch Arabic-first Instagram and TikTok handles. Daily posts in Arabic, English captions secondary. Establish visual identity for MENA: modesty-aware styling, UAE backdrops, local model casting. Content calendar built around Islamic calendar, not Western seasons.

  2. 02 Months 3-4

    Find the voice

    Monthly UAE creator drops with 15-20 influencers per campaign. Focus on modest fashion creators and lifestyle voices with 50K-200K followings. Ramadan capsule storytelling: three limited collections timed to pre-Ramadan, mid-Ramadan, and Eid preparation shopping cycles.

  3. 03 Months 5-6

    Launch in market

    Pre-opening buzz campaigns for flagship Dubai Mall location. Creator store previews, exclusive first-access content for followers. Launch week: Arabic-language press interviews, local media partnerships, influencer store takeovers. Arrive with 50K+ regional followers.
