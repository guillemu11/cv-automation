5. Forecast

How do you build a forecast for e-commerce?

  • Sales history by country, channel and SKU → clean baseline (take out past promos and stockouts)
  • Annual growth projection → phase by month with seasonality: Ramadan, back to school, November (biggest month)
  • + building blocks: launches, promotions and activations, new listings, new customers · − delistings
  • Each block = what similar activations delivered before → incremental, not double counted
  • Compare with target → the gap is what we still need to plan
  • Weekly tracking vs actuals · monthly full analysis and reforecast
  • Category: "if the category grows ten percent and we grow five, we're losing share even though we're growing"

Quién hace qué: "Finance sets the targets, our e-commerce sales manager signs off the number, and I feed in the launches and the campaign calendar." Día a día: plan por quincenas con las ventas de 30 días, tendencia y días de cobertura.

6. Stock y disponibilidad

What do you do when a SKU goes out of stock?

  • Platforms send forecast + PO: Noon & Careem Mon/Thu · talabat Wed → main dark store → other dark stores
  • Two causes: main dark store not replenishing the others (operational talk with the platform) · or the PO didn't cover demand (forecasting talk)
  • My weekly stock-risk review (dashboard we built as a team with Claude): SKUs at risk vs what they ordered → back to the buyer with numbers before it hits zero
  • Web store: raw-material shortage → we took media off those SKUs and pushed the ones we had
  • Rule: no media behind a SKU that isn't available
  • Ask: "Do you measure the eighty-five percent service level at main-warehouse or dark-store level?"

7. Surtido

Have you run an assortment review?

  • Web store myself; platforms with our e-commerce sales team. What sells, what doesn't, cannibalisation
  • Eurocake: 15 SKUs online → 5 tops, mainly in packs and bundles · Flair: 28 → focus on 3 · Befit: 30 → 20 top, 10 medium/low, no cannibalisation so far
  • SMASH: full range, each format has a job: singles for the checkout, packs for Amazon
  • Output: MSL (must-stock list) by brand and channel
  • New market: start with a short list of hero SKUs, earn the range with data

Puente: un Mentos o un Chupa Chups suelto es justo el producto de checkout.

8. JBP con plataformas

How do you build a joint business plan with talabat or Noon?

  • Terms negotiated by our e-commerce sales manager; I build the activity plan: launches, calendar, investment
  • A JBP = shared growth target + joint investment (visibility, promos, media) + service commitment (availability, content) + monthly review
  • SMASH × talabat: every purchase = entry to win talabat cashback cards + creators → daily sales +165%, stayed at double after
  • Control: Befit, same platform, same weeks, no activation → under +5%. That proves it was the campaign

9. Retail media

How do you manage retail media and measure ROI?

  • Monthly budget per platform, ~70/30 media vs promo. Noon gets the most (~40%): each new listing comes with a commitment to spend on ads · 6 launches ≈ 20 listings
  • Formats: hero, search and category banners, mid-page modules, platform Instagram stories (promo budget) + sponsored ads on CPC
  • Amazon: Sponsored Products, auto + manual · Noon: Product Listing Ads (search) + Recommendation Ads (product pages), auto + manual generic/branded
  • Checkout campaigns work best for low-price products → Mentos, Chupa Chups
  • Two levels: ROAS to optimise each campaign (vs its own SKU baseline; Noon's guide for protein 2–3x) · MER = all sell-out ÷ (ads + promo): ≥3x healthy, <2x red flag
  • When a campaign doesn't earn its place we stop it: paused a SMASH recommendation campaign in August returning about half its cost

Si pregunta qué es el MER: "Not profit, not category ROAS. Total sales over total investment. If a product sells 1,000, 500 from ads, and we spend 300 on ads and 50 on promo: ROAS 1.7, MER 2.9."

Si pregunta qué es un buen ROAS: "Depends on the objective: launch 1.5–2x for a limited time; category push 2–3x with MER above 3; best-seller above 3x."

11. Prueba del envío gratis también para "algo que no funcionó"

Tell me about a test you ran / something that didn't work.

  • Free-shipping threshold 300 → 200 AED, 4 weeks vs 4 weeks before, web orders only
  • Criteria set before launch: AOV ≥145 AED · checkout conversion ≥42% · ≥45 orders a month. Rule: 2/3 keep · 1/3 extend · 0/3 revert
  • Orders +22%, revenue flat, AOV −18% (161 → 132) · orders above 300 from 14% to 2% · checkout conversion flat (37% → 36%)
  • 1 of 3 → "The test closes today, and my own rule says to extend it four weeks."
  • Lesson: a threshold sets basket size, it doesn't bring new customers; never judge a test on orders alone
  • Limits: small sample (50–60 orders), soft September, SMASH promo still running in July

Reserva, si pregunta por un dato que estaba mal: el informe de la agencia con el reach estimado desde seguidores → "never present a number I can't rebuild myself."
