Lo que debes poder defender con detalle

Estas afirmaciones del CV y la carta están escritas en el vocabulario del JD. Son ciertas en sustancia, pero un hiring manager que ha construido esta función desde cero y viene de un distribuidor va a rascar. Ten para cada una un caso con nombres, fechas y cifras.

Presupuesto y forecast

"Own annual A&P/budget plans and monthly rolling forecasts jointly with distributors and country teams"

Tu prueba: en Miravia hacías revisiones de surtido con 42 cuentas y abriste 30+ tiendas en dos meses como PIC Fragrances. En DoFreeze, qué SKUs entraron o salieron de talabat o Noon y por qué (rotación, margen, temporada). Lleva un ejemplo con un SKU concreto.

"Build customer / joint business plans with e-retail partners (joint KPIs, promo calendars) and safeguard SKU availability and service levels"

Apertura

Tell me about yourself and your e-commerce experience.

Qué busca: que en 60 segundos vea plataformas, cuentas, cifras y por qué este rol. Termina en Perfetti, no en ti.
Respuesta guía

JBP

How do you build a joint business plan with a platform like talabat or Noon?

Qué busca: su tema favorito. Estructura + ejemplo con cifra + control.
Respuesta guía

I start from the platform's own goals, category growth, basket size, new users, and then put three things on paper: a growth target we both sign, a joint investment plan covering visibility, promo mechanics, sampling and media, and a service commitment on availability and content. Then a monthly review against those KPIs.

A concrete one: for the SMASH launch on talabat we built a mechanic where every purchase in the app was an entry to win talabat cashback cards, amplified with creators. Daily sales went from about 355 to 940 dirhams, plus one hundred sixty-five percent, and stayed at double after the campaign. The important part: our control brand on the same platform in the same window, with no activation, grew under five percent. That's how I like to run JBPs, a shared mechanic, a measured uplift and a control group to prove incrementality.

Forecast

Walk me through how you run a rolling forecast with a distributor, and what you do when availability drops.

Qué busca: viene de Transmed. Quiere cadencia, ownership y una anécdota de rotura de stock. Rellena los huecos con tu dato real.
Respuesta guía

At DoFreeze the forecast runs monthly with sales/ops and the distributor or platform buyer. My input is the commercial calendar: which SKUs we push, when, the expected uplift from campaigns and the promo mechanics, so stock is where the demand is going to be. I also flag when a platform is going to feature us, because a listing that goes out of stock in week one of a campaign wastes the investment.

When availability drops I do three things: pause paid support on that SKU, redirect the campaign to the next-best SKU that is in stock, and get a replenishment date from the partner before switching back on. For example, [caso real: SKU, plataforma, qué pasó, cuánto duró y qué hiciste]. On service level I'd want to understand how PVM measures the eighty-five percent target, at distributor level or at dark-store level, because the levers are different.

Digital shelf

What does a best-in-class product page look like on Noon or talabat, and how do you keep it that way?

Qué busca: él creó programas de content excellence y digital shelf governance. Quiere criterio y disciplina.
Respuesta guía

For confectionery: a title that carries the attributes people search for, brand, variant, format and pack count; a hero image that reads at thumbnail size on a phone; secondary images with the pack open and the size cue; multipacks and bundles as separate listings so we win the basket; and ratings, because on a marketplace a product with no reviews is invisible. Discoverability is half of it: I check share of search for our key terms every week and fix titles and keywords before spending media.

The discipline part is a simple audit: one sheet per platform, availability, content score and search rank per SKU, reviewed monthly with the partner. On Noon our hero SKU, the sugar-free marble cakes, did over six thousand units in a single seasonal campaign because the page and the search terms were ready before the media went live.

Retail media

How do you manage retail media and sponsored ads, and how do you measure ROI?

Qué busca: lo gestiona él mismo. Sé exacta sobre qué has hecho en plataforma y qué en Meta/Google. No mezcles.
Respuesta guía

Two layers. Off-platform, I run Meta and Google with ROAS and conversion as the decision metrics, testing creatives and audiences and cutting what doesn't pay back. On-platform, [tu experiencia real con sponsored listings en Noon o talabat: qué formatos, qué presupuesto, qué resultado]. What I'd bring is the same rule in both: always-on only on SKUs with availability and a good page, budget weighted to the seasonal peaks, and every campaign with a baseline so we can talk about incremental sales, not just clicks.

I'm also strict with reporting. I audited an agency report last year where reach was estimated from followers, not measured, and the same creator was counted twice with a five-x difference. We changed the measurement, and found that micro-creators were performing ten to forty times the campaign average. Retail media reporting has the same traps, and I'd rather have a smaller number that's true.

Motivación
