Across much of MENA, roughly half the population is under 25 — one of the youngest, fastest-growing consumer bases on earth.

For a toys portfolio built on the most-loved heroes in the world, that isn't just a market. It's a whole generation waiting to meet them — and a category waiting to be built the right way.

The thesis

Kids here already know Elsa, already love Spider-Man, already dream in lightsabers. The gap isn't demand — it's availability, relevance and timing.

A winning Toys category in MENA is built partner by partner: the right licensees, the right shelves, and the right hero at the right cultural moment — Eid gifting, back-to-school, a movie weekend, a National Day.

My job as Category Manager is to turn that love into a plan — an annual operating plan, quarterly forecasts and joint business plans that put the right hero in the right home, every single season.

Six pillars to build the category

How the vision becomes an operating plan.

01 Category vision & AOP

The annual plan

Build the MENA Hardlines Toys annual operating plan and quarterly forecasts by franchise and market, set clear licensee objectives, and translate the numbers into a working growth plan.

02 Licensees & partnerships

The right partners

Develop new and existing licensee relationships, lead joint business planning and regional contract negotiation, and secure high-impact partners and listings across the region.

03 Franchise & culture moments

Hero at the right moment

Sync toy ranges to tentpole franchise moments and the MENA cultural calendar — Ramadan & Eid gifting, back-to-school, National Days — so the right character lands exactly when families are buying.

04 Retail magic · brick & mortar

Shelves that tell stories

Hero end-caps, in-store theatre and gifting destinations with modern-trade partners — turning the toy aisle from a shelf into an experience, grounded in real visual-merchandising craft.

05 Digital, social & retail media

Where families already are

Human-first social, creator and UGC activation plus always-on content and retail media that drives discovery and demand straight to partner shelves and e-tail.

06 Product & insight

Made for the region

Work with creative teams and licensees on regionally-relevant product and consumer insight, spot scalable high-impact opportunities, and champion supplier integrity (ILS) end-to-end.
