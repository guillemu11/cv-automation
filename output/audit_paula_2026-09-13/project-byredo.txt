THE MANIFESTO

In the Gulf, fragrance is personal, generous and remembered.

A client who is known — their scent, their story, their occasions — doesn't compare prices. They come back, and they bring people.

Known by Name turns every Byredo door into a relationship engine: a segmented client base, a fragrance-profiling ritual, and a team that remembers. Built on a clienteling programme I ran in beauty, and on years working the Gulf's leading Arabian and oud houses.

The playbook
01
A client base, not a till

Build a quality, segmented CRM database across doors — so high-value clients are recognised, followed up and grown for lifetime value, not counted once at checkout.

02
The fragrance-profiling ritual

A consistent scent-discovery experience across every door and associate — the in-store moment that turns a browse into an emotional, high-conversion relationship.

03
Fluent in oud

An assortment and service language that speaks to the Gulf consumer — informed by working the official distributors of Arabian Oud, Lattafa, Swiss Arabian and Ajmal.

04
One commercial view

Retail and wholesale doors read as a single P&L — sell-through, basket and margin on one scorecard, so activation moves to where it protects profitability and growth.
